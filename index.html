Case Study 01

How I Cut Wasted Ad Spend by 48% for a B2B Telehealth SaaS Brand

The budget stayed the same. I just spent it differently, across Google, Meta, and LinkedIn.

The Challenge

Same underlying problem across all three platforms, three different symptoms.

Meta
  • Ads were running broad creator content, not built around any specific segment of their buyer
  • Same message going out to everyone, so it pulled in a lot of people who were never going to convert
  • The lead cost itself wasn't really the problem, the leads just weren't the right fit
  • Sales team was spending real time, sometimes weeks, just figuring out who was even worth following up with
LinkedIn
  • No job title or company targeting set up at all, campaigns were running fully broad
  • Creative was basically recycled Facebook style, hook driven ads, which doesn't work the same way on LinkedIn
  • Ads had no real offer, just generic messaging with nothing for someone to actually click for
  • $5,200/month going out every month, and the channel was producing close to zero usable leads
Google Ads
  • Campaign structure had overlapping issues, so it was hard to tell which campaign was actually driving results
  • All traffic was being sent to the homepage instead of a dedicated landing page, so there was no clear next step for anyone who clicked
  • Their own brand name searches, people already looking for them by name, weren't converting even though the demand was clearly there
  • A meaningful chunk of the ~$11,000/month budget was going to broad keywords with no real relevance to what they offer
  • Cost per lead was sitting at $170, well above what the business needed it to be

The Solution

The fix started with the buyer, not the platform. I broke the single broad audience into specific personas, startups, clinics, and health brands, and treated each one as its own campaign instead of a variation of the same ad.

Meta
  • Built a distinct ad and message for each segment, instead of one generic message trying to cover all of them
  • Kept spend focused on prospecting new people, not leaning on retargeting to make the numbers look better than they were
LinkedIn
  • Pulled the recycled Facebook style hook creative, it clearly wasn't landing on this platform
  • Rebuilt every ad around context and a clear offer, explaining what we were offering and why it mattered before ever asking for the click
  • Switched the campaign objective from awareness to conversion
  • Added retargeting as a support layer, not the main play
Google Ads
  • Audited where the budget was actually going and cut searches with no relevance to the business
  • Built a negative keyword list to stop the bleeding
  • Launched a dedicated brand campaign so the business stopped losing people already searching for them by name
  • Moved traffic off the homepage and onto a dedicated landing page built around the actual offer
  • Cleaned up the overlapping campaign structure so the numbers could finally be trusted

  • Same total budget across all three channels, but working harder

  • A repeatable system across three channels, not a one time lucky campaign
